Adapted for cricket: muscle::String was replaced with Haiku's BString, the
automatic reachability probe was made optional per instance, and the status
messages were reworded for DCC. Used by dcc.cpp to forward cricket's DCC
listen ports on the home router (PCP / NAT-PMP / UPnP IGD). The changes are
likewise public domain.
